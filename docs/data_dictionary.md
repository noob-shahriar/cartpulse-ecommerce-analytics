## Relationships

```
customers 1 --- N orders 1 --- N order_items N --- 1 products N --- 1 category_translation
                    |  \                        N --- 1 sellers
                    |   \-- 1 --- N order_payments
                    \------ 1 --- N order_reviews (mostly 1, sometimes >1)
```

- `customer_id` is unique per order; **`customer_unique_id`** is the true customer (used for repeat-purchase analysis).
- Revenue fields: `price` (product value), `freight_value` (shipping charged), `payment_value` (cash actually paid).
- **There is no product cost or profit field** anywhere in the dataset.
